## What is Nail Business Operator?

Nail Business Operator is a reusable AI skill designed specifically around the day-to-day decisions involved in running a nail business.

Instead of giving generic business advice, it helps turn questions into:

* Pricing decisions
* Revenue calculations
* Booking improvements
* Rebooking systems
* Promotions
* Client messages
* Service menus
* Inventory systems
* Salon SOPs
* Weekly business reviews
* Measurable experiments

The goal is simple:

> Turn your AI assistant into a practical nail-business operations partner.

# What It Can Do

## 💰 Pricing & Profit

Ask:

> My gel manicure is $45 and takes 75 minutes. Help me evaluate the price.

The skill can evaluate:

* Revenue per booked hour
* Product costs
* Appointment duration
* Price increases
* Add-ons
* Premium services
* Service-menu positioning

---

## 📅 Booking & Capacity

Ask:

> I'm booked Friday and Saturday but Tuesday and Wednesday stay empty. What should I change?

The skill looks at:

* Available service hours
* Booked hours
* Schedule gaps
* Peak periods
* Cancellation patterns
* Appointment duration
* Rebooking

It focuses on the actual bottleneck instead of automatically saying:

> Post more on Instagram.

# Example Prompts

```text
I am an independent nail tech.

Gel manicure: $50
Average appointment: 70 minutes
Approximate product cost: $6

Structured gel manicure: $70
Average appointment: 100 minutes
Approximate product cost: $9

Analyze my service pricing and tell me which service deserves attention first.
```


# How the Skill Thinks

The skill first tries to identify whether the real constraint is:

```text
Demand
   ↓
Conversion
   ↓
Pricing
   ↓
Capacity
   ↓
Retention
   ↓
Operations
```

This matters because an empty schedule and a full-but-unprofitable schedule require very different solutions.

---

# Core Business Metrics

When the numbers are available, Nail Business Operator can work with metrics such as:

### Revenue Per Booked Hour

```text
service price ÷ service duration in hours
```

### Booked Utilization

```text
booked service hours ÷ available service hours × 100
```

### Revenue Per Available Hour

```text
service revenue ÷ available service hours
```

### Rebooking Rate

```text
clients booking their next appointment
÷
completed clients
× 100
```

### Repeat Client Rate

```text
returning clients ÷ total clients × 100
```

# Safety

Nail Business Operator is a business tool.

It does not:

* Diagnose nail conditions
* Diagnose skin conditions
* Treat medical conditions
* Replace professional medical guidance
* Invent salon regulations
* Invent licensing rules
* Provide personalized legal advice
* Provide personalized tax advice

Salon regulations, sanitation requirements, employment rules, taxes, licensing, and insurance requirements may vary by location.

Verify jurisdiction-specific requirements with an appropriate authoritative source.
