# Sponsor Nail Business Operator 💅

Nail Business Operator is an open-source AI skill built to help independent nail techs and salon owners make better business decisions.

The core project is free.

Sponsorship helps me continue building practical tools around it.

## What sponsorship funds

Your support helps fund development of:

* Nail pricing calculators
* Appointment profitability tools
* Rebooking workflows
* Weekly business dashboards
* Client communication templates
* Inventory systems
* Salon SOPs
* Documentation
* Testing
* Integrations
* Community-requested features

## Why sponsor?

Running a nail business involves much more than creating great nails.

Owners have to make decisions about pricing, scheduling, inventory, clients, marketing, retention, staffing, and profitability—often without dedicated operations support.

This project is intended to make those decisions easier.

If the project saves you time, improves your workflow, or helps your salon, sponsorship is one way to help keep it maintained and growing.
